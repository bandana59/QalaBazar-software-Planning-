Qala Bazar
 E-Commerce Marketplace for Nepali Art and Handicrafts

Qala Bazar is a specialized e-commerce platform designed to connect Nepali artists, craftspeople, and customers in one digital marketplace.

The platform focuses on products that represent Nepali creativity and culture, including paintings, wood carvings, traditional arts, handicrafts, sculptures, handmade products, and other cultural artwork.

Instead of requiring artists to depend only on physical shops, exhibitions, or social media to reach customers, Qala Bazar provides a dedicated online space where they can showcase their work and sell their products.
---
 Project Overview

Qala Bazar is designed around three main users:

Customers– Browse artwork, search for products, view artist profiles, add products to a cart, and place orders.
Artists/Sellers – Create artist profiles, upload and manage products, manage prices and stock, and process customer orders.
  Administrators – Manage users, artists, products, categories, orders, and overall platform activities.

The main goal is to create a marketplace where Nepali art is easier to discover and artists have a wider digital market for their work.

---

 Problem Statement

Many Nepali artists and craftspeople produce unique artwork and traditional products, but reaching customers can be difficult when they depend mainly on physical stores, exhibitions, personal networks, or social media.

At the same time, customers interested in Nepali artwork may have difficulty finding different artists and authentic handmade products in one place.

Qala Bazar addresses this problem by providing a centralized online marketplace specifically focused on Nepali art and craftsmanship.

---

 Objectives

The main objectives of Qala Bazar are to:

Provide artists with an online platform to showcase their work.
  Help customers discover Nepali artwork and handmade products.
  Make purchasing Nepali art more convenient.
  Provide artists with tools to manage their products and orders.
  Create a structured marketplace for different categories of Nepali art and crafts.

 Example Use Case

Consider an artist who creates traditional Nepali wood carvings.

The artist can register on Qala Bazar and create an artist profile. They can then upload photographs of their wood carvings, provide descriptions, set prices, and specify available stock.

A customer searching for traditional Nepali woodwork can find the product, view information about the artist, add the carving to their shopping cart, and place an order.

The artist can then view the order from their dashboard and update its status as it is processed.

This creates a direct digital connection between the artist and customer.
